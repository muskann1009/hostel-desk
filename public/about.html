About HostelDesk

Built so complaints don't get lost on a notice board

Before HostelDesk, most hostel issues were reported on paper slips pinned to a warden's door, or shouted across a corridor. Some got fixed fast. Many were forgotten. HostelDesk gives every complaint a paper trail — from the moment it's filed to the moment it's resolved.

What it's for

Any student living on campus can log an issue with their room, washroom, mess, Wi-Fi or hostel facilities, and track exactly where it stands — without needing to ask around.

Who it's for

Students filing complaints, wardens triaging them, and maintenance staff closing them out. Everyone looks at the same record instead of three different notebooks.

What we handle

If it affects your room or common areas, it belongs here.

Electrical & plumbing

Fans, lights, sockets, taps, drains and geysers — the things that make a room livable.

Furniture & fixtures

Broken beds, chairs, cupboards, windows and doors.

Wi-Fi, mess & security

Connectivity issues, food quality concerns, and anything affecting hostel safety.

Ready to report something?

It takes less time than finding the warden's office hours.
